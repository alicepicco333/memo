The Meme Ontology

MEMO models 5,000 Know Your Meme entries as an OWL 2 knowledge graph. Master’s thesis by Alice Picco, Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge, University of Bologna (supervisor Fabio Vitali); begun at the Institute of Network Cultures, Amsterdam.
