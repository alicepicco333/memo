Eight transformation dimensions
CaptionChange

Only the superimposed caption text changes, image substrate unchanged.

VisualSubstrate

The original image has been subjected to deliberate degradation, heavy compression artefacts, extreme saturation manipulation or distortion (the so-called fried meme aesthetic), preserving the template's formal structure while corrupting or altering its visual quality.

MediumShift

The representational medium changes: photographic to drawn or vice versa.

StyleShift

Visual style changes without a full medium shift: the visual register changes but the representational medium remains the same.

CompositionShift

Elements added, removed or repositioned within the frame.

CrossoverMerge

Characters or visual elements from another identifiable meme template or fictional intellectual property (film, television series, video game, book, or other media franchise) are visually integrated into the image. Caption-only references to external IP do not qualify.

LanguageShift

Caption language changes while visual content is preserved.

Localization

Template adapted for a specific cultural context beyond translation.

Datasets

Three interrelated datasets used in the Meme Ontology project.

Global dataset

5,000 memes sourced from Know Your Meme, enriched with CLIP-based image classification and structured using the Meme Ontology OWL ontology.

D0: annotated sample

50 memes sampled from the global dataset with enriched metadata: descriptions, view counts, tags, and full ontology property annotations including image type, format, platform, region and time period.

Variant dataset

Meme variants scraped from Know Your Meme. A subset of 50 variants is annotated with transformation dimensions, extents, captions, and cultural references.
